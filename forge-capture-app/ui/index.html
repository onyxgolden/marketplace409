    FORGE Capture help

    Capture

    Pick Image, Video or Meeting, choose what to grab — Region (drag a rectangle), Window, Full screen, or Scrolling — then hit the red Capture button.

    • Region freezes the screen, then shows crosshairs and a magnifier so you can aim precisely. Drag to select; the live readout shows pixel coordinates and size.
    • Delay waits 3–10 s before capturing. Cursor includes the mouse pointer.
    • Scrolling lives under Advanced: pick a region or window and an engine, then start/stop the run.

    Shortcuts

    • PrintScreen — open Capture
    • Shift+PrintScreen — start a region capture
    • Ctrl+PrintScreen — arm window capture
    • Alt+PrintScreen — capture the current monitor
    • Esc / right-click — cancel the region picker

    AI Edit

    Every captured image has an AI Edit button. Describe the change; the original is never modified — the finished edit comes back as a new versioned copy (e.g. …-ai-edit.png).

    Capture itself never touches the network: it writes your image and prompt to a local job folder and waits. An AI runner carries jobs to the AI team and drops results back. Until a runner is set up, jobs wait honestly in the queue — see forge-capture-app/docs/ai-edit.md for the setup contract.

    Meeting mode

    The Meeting tab records microphone audio locally and queues a transcription job the same honest way: the audio file and a job.json wait in the local job folder until a transcription runner (local Whisper) picks them up and drops result.json back. The transcript appears with timestamps, search and copy; the original audio is never modified. If the app crashes mid-recording, the partial audio is recovered and queued on the next launch — see forge-capture-app/docs/meeting-mode.md for the runner contract.

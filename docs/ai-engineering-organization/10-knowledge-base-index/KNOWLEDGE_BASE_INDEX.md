## Record Naming Standard

Use descriptive uppercase filenames for authoritative documents.

Examples:

- ADR-0001-CANONICAL-INTELLIGENCE-BOUNDARY.md
- INCIDENT-20260726-RSC-SERIALIZATION.md
- PATTERN-SERVER-CLIENT-SERIALIZATION.md
- PROJECT-FORGE-STATUS.md

## Knowledge Entry Requirements

Each knowledge entry should identify:

- title,
- category,
- project,
- status,
- source,
- verification date,
- related files,
- related commits,
- superseded records.

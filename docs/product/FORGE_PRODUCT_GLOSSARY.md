# FORGE Product Glossary

## Purpose

Provide canonical definitions for product terminology used throughout FORGE.

## Core Principles

Every important term has exactly one definition.

Product terminology should remain stable across documentation, engineering, AI agents, and customer communications.

## Foundational Terms

### North Star
The permanent long-term mission and vision for FORGE.

### Product Operating System (FPOS)
The governance system describing how products are researched, evaluated, designed, prioritized, implemented, and evolved.

### Product Constitution
The permanent principles governing product decisions.

### Product Decision Record (PDR)
A documented explanation of an important product decision and its rationale.

### Vision Traceability
The ability to connect every implemented capability back to the North Star.

### Shared Platform Capability
A capability intended for reuse across multiple FORGE products rather than belonging to a single application.

### Product Area
A major customer-facing domain such as Rental Operations, Financial, Marketplace, Academy, or Community.

### Opportunity
An evidence-based recommendation that may improve customer outcomes.

### Opportunity Center
The customer experience for discovering rebates, incentives, savings opportunities, trusted providers, and recommendations.

### Opportunity Network
The ecosystem connecting customers, contractors, manufacturers, service providers, and partners through trusted opportunities.

### Property Passport
The permanent operational, financial, maintenance, warranty, and lifecycle history of a property.

### Asset Passport
The reusable lifecycle record for any managed asset.

### Feature Investment Card
A structured evaluation of customer value, engineering effort, cost, risk, and strategic impact.

## Rule

New platform terminology should be added here before becoming part of the broader documentation.

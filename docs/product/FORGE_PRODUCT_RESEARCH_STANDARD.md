# FORGE Product Research Standard

## Purpose

Define the mandatory research process before any major FORGE product or module is designed.

## Objectives

Research exists to understand:

- Customer problems
- Existing market solutions
- Workflow strengths
- Workflow weaknesses
- Competitive opportunities
- Shared platform reuse
- Long-term strategic fit

Research is not feature collection.

Research identifies customer outcomes.

---

# Standard Research Sections

## 1. Market Overview

- Product
- Company
- Target customers
- Pricing
- Market position

## 2. User Journey

Describe complete user workflows.

Do not list features.

## 3. UX Evaluation

Evaluate:

- Simplicity
- Navigation
- Click count
- Learnability
- Mobile experience

## 4. Customer Feedback

Document:

- Praise
- Complaints
- Missing capabilities
- Frequently requested improvements

## 5. Competitive Strengths

What should FORGE adopt?

## 6. Competitive Weaknesses

What should FORGE avoid?

## 7. FORGE Opportunities

Where can FORGE become meaningfully better?

## 8. Shared Platform Reuse

Can this capability reuse:

- Billing
- Asset Passport
- Opportunity Network
- Financial Events
- Marketplace
- Community
- AI
- Authentication

## 9. Product Recommendation

One of:

- Adopt
- Improve
- Invent
- Reject

---

## Guiding Rule

Research explains why users succeed or struggle.

It does not simply describe software.

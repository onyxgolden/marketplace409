# FORGE North Star

**Status:** Foundational Draft
**Purpose:** Permanent expression of the total FORGE product vision

## Mission

FORGE exists to help people make better financial, property, asset, and business decisions with less effort by combining trustworthy intelligence, excellent product design, community participation, and disciplined engineering.

## North-Star Outcome

FORGE should help users:

- understand what happened;
- understand why it happened;
- identify what may happen next;
- evaluate available actions;
- estimate financial consequences;
- act through trusted providers and workflows;
- preserve the resulting history for future decisions.

## Core Product Philosophy

### Invisible Complexity

FORGE may be sophisticated internally, but the user experience should remain understandable and action oriented.

### Product Before Technology

Users buy outcomes, not architecture.

### Decision-Centered Design

FORGE should be designed around the decisions customers need to make, not merely around records they need to store.

### Total Vision

FORGE is a connected intelligence platform serving:

- landlords and property managers;
- homeowners and investors;
- contractors and service businesses;
- entrepreneurs and small businesses;
- families and young people learning financial literacy;
- accountants, tax professionals, vendors, and trusted partners.

## Platform Vision

FORGE combines:

- Financial Intelligence
- Rental Operations
- Property Intelligence
- Asset Intelligence
- Opportunity Intelligence
- Tax Intelligence
- Business Intelligence
- Marketplace and Contractor Networks
- Financial Literacy and Training
- Community-Driven Product Evolution
- FORGE Engineering and AI Orchestration

## Guiding Rule

The complete vision must remain visible while individual capabilities are implemented incrementally.

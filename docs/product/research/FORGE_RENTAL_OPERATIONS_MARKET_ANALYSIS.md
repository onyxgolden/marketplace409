# FORGE Rental Operations Market Analysis

**Status:** Research Draft

## Market Overview

Rental operations software helps property owners and managers coordinate the activities required to operate rental assets.

Common capabilities include:

- Leasing workflows
- Rent collection
- Tenant communication
- Maintenance coordination
- Accounting
- Reporting
- Compliance support

The market serves multiple operating models:

- Individual landlords managing a small number of properties.
- Small portfolio owners scaling rental operations.
- Professional property managers managing properties for owners.
- Larger operators requiring automation and operational controls.

Customers generally adopt these systems to reduce administrative work, improve visibility, organize workflows, and make better property decisions.

## Core Customer Problems

Across rental operations, customers commonly experience:

### Limited Financial Visibility

Owners often know income and expenses occurred but struggle to understand:

- why profitability changed;
- which properties are performing well;
- where money is being lost;
- what decisions should happen next.

### Manual Administrative Work

Common time-consuming activities include:

- collecting payments;
- tracking expenses;
- coordinating maintenance;
- updating records;
- communicating with tenants.

### Fragmented Information

Rental information is often distributed across:

- bank accounts;
- spreadsheets;
- accounting systems;
- emails;
- text messages;
- maintenance records.

### Reactive Maintenance Management

Many owners discover problems after costs increase.

Challenges include:

- delayed repairs;
- unclear priorities;
- incomplete property history;
- difficulty evaluating vendors.

### Scaling Complexity

As portfolios grow, owners need:

- consistent workflows;
- better reporting;
- automation;
- confidence that important tasks are not missed.

### Decision Support Gap

Many systems record activity but provide limited help answering:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should I do next?
- What will be the financial impact?

## Industry Trends

The rental operations market is changing as owners and managers expect more visibility, automation, and convenience.

### Increasing Professionalization of Small Landlords

More individual owners are operating rental properties as businesses.

This increases demand for:

- better financial visibility;
- standardized processes;
- reporting;
- operational efficiency.

### Growth of Digital Payments

Rent collection continues moving toward digital workflows.

Customers increasingly expect:

- online payments;
- payment history;
- automated reminders;
- transaction visibility.

### Demand for Integrated Workflows

Owners increasingly prefer fewer disconnected systems.

Important integrations include:

- banking;
- accounting;
- maintenance;
- communication;
- vendor services.

### Focus on Operational Efficiency

Property managers are seeking ways to reduce manual work through:

- automation;
- workflow tools;
- centralized information;
- better reporting.

### Increased Importance of Data

Rental owners have more operational data than before but often lack systems that convert information into useful decisions.

The market is moving from simple record keeping toward better visibility and actionable insights.

# FORGE Rental Operations Research Plan

**Status:** Research Initiation

## Purpose

Define the research approach for evaluating the Rental Operations opportunity within the FORGE platform.

The purpose of this research is not to copy existing property management software.

The purpose is to understand:

- customer problems;
- existing workflows;
- market strengths;
- customer frustrations;
- opportunities where FORGE can create differentiated value.

---

# Research Objective

Determine whether FORGE should enter the Rental Operations market and identify the highest-value capabilities to build.

---

# Strategic Alignment

Rental Operations must support the FORGE North Star:

FORGE helps users:

- understand what happened;
- understand why it happened;
- identify what may happen next;
- evaluate available actions;
- estimate financial consequences;
- act through trusted workflows;
- preserve history for future decisions.

---

# Target Customers

Research will evaluate:

## Individual Landlords

Questions:

- How do they manage properties today?
- What decisions are difficult?
- What tasks consume the most time?

## Small Portfolio Owners

Questions:

- How do workflows change with scale?
- Where does complexity increase?
- What reporting is needed?

## Professional Property Managers

Questions:

- What operational systems are required?
- Where does automation provide value?
- What limitations exist in current platforms?

## Tenants

Questions:

- What creates friction?
- What improves satisfaction?
- What communication problems exist?

## Contractors and Service Providers

Questions:

- How are maintenance relationships managed?
- What opportunities exist for marketplace integration?

---

# Competitive Research Scope

Initial competitors:

- Buildium
- AppFolio
- RentRedi
- Avail
- TurboTenant
- DoorLoop

Additional platforms may be added as research progresses.

---

# Research Evaluation Framework

Each platform will be evaluated using:

## Market Overview

- Company
- Product position
- Target users
- Pricing model

## User Journey

Evaluate complete workflows:

- property setup;
- tenant onboarding;
- rent collection;
- maintenance;
- accounting;
- reporting;
- decision making.

## Customer Feedback

Capture:

- customer praise;
- customer complaints;
- missing capabilities;
- workflow friction.

## Competitive Analysis

Identify:

- strengths;
- weaknesses;
- opportunities.

## FORGE Opportunity Analysis

Evaluate:

- Adopt
- Improve
- Invent
- Reject

---

# Shared Platform Evaluation

Every opportunity should evaluate reuse of:

- Financial Events
- Financial Workspace
- Billing Platform
- Property Passport
- Asset Passport
- Marketplace
- AI Services
- Authentication
- Community Platform

---

# Completion Criteria

Research phase is complete when FORGE has:

- documented market understanding;
- documented customer workflows;
- competitor analysis;
- opportunity map;
- initial feature investment cards;
- recorded product recommendations.

---

# Guiding Principle

Research explains why users succeed or struggle.

It does not simply describe software.

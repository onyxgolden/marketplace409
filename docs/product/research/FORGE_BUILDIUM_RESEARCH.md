# FORGE Buildium Research

**Status:** Research Draft

## Product

Buildium

## Vendor

Buildium, a property management software platform focused on residential property managers and owners.

## Target Customer

Primary customers:

- Small to mid-sized property management companies.
- Owners managing multiple rental properties.
- Professional managers needing accounting and operational workflows.

## User Journey

Typical workflows include:

- Create and manage properties.
- Maintain tenant records.
- Market vacancies.
- Process rental applications.
- Manage leases.
- Collect rent payments.
- Track accounting activity.
- Coordinate maintenance.
- Provide owner reporting.

## Customer Praise

Commonly recognized strengths:

- Broad property management workflow coverage.
- Strong accounting capabilities.
- Established market presence.
- Designed for professional property managers.

## Customer Complaints

Common concerns reported by users include:

- Learning curve due to feature depth.
- Reporting flexibility limitations for some workflows.
- Cost concerns as portfolios grow.
- Desire for more modern automation experiences.

## Best Workflows

Strong areas:

- Property accounting.
- Rent collection workflows.
- Tenant management.
- Owner reporting.

## Weakest Workflows

Potential friction areas:

- Advanced customization.
- Simplified decision support.
- Turning operational data into recommendations.

## Competitive Advantages

Buildium provides:

- Mature property management workflows.
- Industry familiarity.
- Comprehensive operational coverage.

## Competitive Weaknesses

Potential gaps:

- Limited intelligence layer beyond recorded data.
- Less emphasis on predictive recommendations.
- Opportunity for simpler decision experiences.

## Opportunities for FORGE

Potential FORGE opportunities:

- Combine financial events with property operations.
- Explain why performance changed.
- Provide evidence-backed recommendations.
- Connect maintenance, financial impact, and asset value.

## Shared Platform Capabilities

Potential reuse:

- Financial Events.
- Financial Workspace.
- Property Passport.
- Asset Intelligence.
- AI Services.
- Marketplace.

## Recommended Actions

- Adopt proven property workflows.
- Improve decision visibility.
- Invent intelligence capabilities.
- Reject unnecessary feature duplication.

## Overall Recommendation

Research Further.

Buildium demonstrates strong operational foundations. FORGE should study successful workflows while focusing differentiation on financial intelligence, property intelligence, and decision support.

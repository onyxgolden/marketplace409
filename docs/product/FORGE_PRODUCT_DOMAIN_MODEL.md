# FORGE Product Domain Model

**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Purpose

The Product Domain Model defines the major business domains that collectively make up the FORGE platform.

Every capability, roadmap item, customer journey, research initiative, engineering effort, and AI work assignment should belong to one or more product domains.

The domain model provides the highest-level view of the FORGE product ecosystem.

# Guiding Principles

- Every capability belongs to at least one domain.
- Domains own distinct business responsibilities.
- Shared capabilities should minimize duplication.
- Customer journeys may span multiple domains.
- AI agents operate within explicit domain boundaries.
- Research should be organized by domain.
- Engineering implementation follows approved product domains.
- FORGE OS coordinates execution but does not own product-domain business rules.

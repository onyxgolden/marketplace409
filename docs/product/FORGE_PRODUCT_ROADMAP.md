# FORGE Product Roadmap

**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Purpose

The FORGE Product Roadmap describes the planned evolution of the platform from foundational architecture through production maturity.

Unlike a feature backlog, the roadmap is organized around the canonical product domains defined by the Product Domain Model.

Every roadmap initiative should trace back to one or more capabilities defined in the Platform Capabilities catalog.

---

## Roadmap Principles

The roadmap follows these principles:

- Research precedes implementation.
- Product architecture drives engineering architecture.
- Every capability belongs to a canonical product domain.
- Cross-domain collaboration is preferred over duplication.
- Decision Intelligence evolves across every business domain.
- FORGE OS evolves as the governed execution platform.
- Production readiness is required before general availability.

# Roadmap Governance

The FORGE Product Roadmap is governed by the following principles.

## Capability First

Every roadmap item must reference one or more capabilities defined in:

- FORGE Platform Capabilities

No capability should appear on the roadmap before it exists in the capability catalog.

## Research First

Major initiatives begin with research.

Research validates:

- customer problems;
- business value;
- implementation alternatives;
- architectural alignment.

## Domain Ownership

Every roadmap initiative belongs to one primary product domain.

Cross-domain work is coordinated through explicit interfaces while preserving canonical ownership.

## Engineering Alignment

Engineering architecture follows approved product architecture.

Engineering implementation should trace to:

- Product Domain
- Capability Area
- Capability
- Engineering Component
- Repository Implementation
- Validation Evidence

## Decision Intelligence

Decision Intelligence evolves across every roadmap phase but never becomes the authoritative owner of operational business information.

## FORGE OS

FORGE OS evolves alongside every product domain by providing governed execution, AI coordination, automation, lifecycle management, evidence, and observability.

---

# External Dependencies

Some roadmap milestones depend on external organizations.

Examples include:

- Banking provider approvals
- Payment processor approvals
- Marketplace partnerships
- Third-party integrations
- Regulatory approvals

These dependencies may affect delivery schedules without changing product priorities.

---

# Success Measures

The roadmap is considered successful when:

- Every product domain reaches production maturity.
- Every capability traces to the Platform Capabilities catalog.
- Customer journeys map directly to implemented capabilities.
- Engineering architecture remains aligned with product architecture.
- AI workers understand domain ownership and governance.
- FORGE OS coordinates intelligent execution across the complete platform.
- The platform supports long-term evolution without sacrificing architectural consistency.

---

# Guiding Principle

Deliver FORGE through disciplined, domain-driven evolution where research informs capabilities, capabilities drive the roadmap, engineering implements approved architecture, and FORGE OS governs intelligent execution across the platform.

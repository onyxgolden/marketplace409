# FORGE Product Decisions

**Status:** Active Register

## PDR-001 — Product Engineering Is a First-Class Discipline

Product strategy, customer outcomes, UX, and market intelligence require distinct ownership from software architecture.

## PDR-002 — Preserve the Total Vision

Individual feature work must remain traceable to the complete FORGE platform vision.

## PDR-003 — Separate Billing from Product Domains

Billing is a shared platform capability that may serve Rental Operations, Marketplace, FORGE Financial, and future products.

## PDR-004 — Recommendations Remain Independent

Commercial relationships, sponsorships, rebates, and discounts must not alter the underlying recommendation.

## PDR-005 — Strategic Ideas Must Be Version Controlled

No strategic product idea is considered preserved until it exists in repository documentation.

## PDR-006 — Community Demand Is Evidence, Not Authority

Votes and followers influence prioritization but do not override strategy, safety, cost, architecture, or maintainability.

## PDR-007 — Feature Cost Must Include Lifetime Cost

Product analysis includes implementation, infrastructure, support, compliance, vendor, and maintenance costs.

## PDR-008 — Asset Intelligence Is Shared

Property equipment, vehicles, tools, and other durable assets should reuse a canonical Asset Passport model where practical.

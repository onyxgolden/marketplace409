# FORGE Research

## Purpose

The Research layer validates architectural theory before implementation.

Research answers questions such as:

- Can this capability be built?
- Which APIs exist?
- What licensing or legal considerations apply?
- What are the technical constraints?
- What are the expected costs?

Research does **not** modify the implementation roadmap.

Research supports informed engineering decisions by validating ideas before they become approved implementation work.

Typical research topics include:

- External APIs
- Third-party integrations
- Government data sources
- Financial institution capabilities
- Regulatory considerations
- Cost analysis
- Technical feasibility

Successful research may graduate into future roadmap phases after architectural review and approval.

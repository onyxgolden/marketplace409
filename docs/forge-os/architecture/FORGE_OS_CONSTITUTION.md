# FORGE OS Constitution

Status: Draft
Version: 1.0.0
Authority: Owner Approved
Scope: Governance of the FORGE OS Platform

---

# Preamble

The FORGE OS Constitution is the highest engineering authority of the platform.

Its purpose is to preserve the architectural integrity, governance, and long-term evolution of FORGE OS.

This document defines enduring engineering law rather than implementation strategy.

---

# Article I — Purpose

The Constitution exists to:

- establish enduring engineering principles;
- define architectural authority;
- protect platform integrity;
- govern architectural evolution.

---

# Article II — Authority

The authority of engineering governance shall be:

1. Constitution
2. Architectural Principles
3. Architecture
4. Architectural Decision Records
5. Specifications
6. Implementation

A lower authority shall not knowingly contradict a higher authority.

---

# Article III — Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall remain true unless amended through constitutional governance.

- The Kernel remains intentionally minimal.
- Platform boundaries are enforced through stable contracts.
- Engineering knowledge is canonical.
- Governance precedes irreversible change.
- Validation evidence precedes acceptance.
- Workspaces remain isolated.
- Managers remain independently bounded.
- Authority flows downward.
- Evidence flows upward.

---

# Article IV — Engineering Governance

Engineering decisions shall be classified as one of:

- Constitutional Invariant
- Architectural Decision
- Engineering Experiment
- Implementation Detail

Each classification carries a different level of stability and review.

---

# Article V — Evidence

When certainty is unavailable, FORGE shall prefer small, reversible experiments that generate measurable engineering evidence.

Evidence—not opinion—shall guide architectural evolution.

---

# Article VI — Stewardship

Every accepted change should improve at least one of:

- clarity
- maintainability
- testability
- observability
- architectural consistency

---

# Article VII — Amendments

Constitutional amendments require explicit architectural review and owner approval.

No implementation may redefine constitutional authority through practice alone.

---

# Definitions

Constitution
: Highest engineering authority.

Architecture
: Structural design of the platform.

Experiment
: A reversible implementation intended to produce evidence.

Evidence
: Objective information used to validate engineering decisions.

---

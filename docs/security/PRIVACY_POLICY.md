# Privacy Policy

## Document Control

- Organization: 409 Marketplace LLC
- Platform: 409 Marketplace / FORGE
- Document Status: Active
- Policy Owner: Jason Morgan
- Review Frequency: Annual and after material privacy changes

---

## Overview

409 Marketplace respects user privacy and is committed to protecting
information provided through the platform.

This policy describes how 409 Marketplace collects, uses, protects, and
handles information required to provide application functionality.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to information processed through:

- 409 Marketplace applications.
- Financial integrations.
- Authentication systems.
- Payment workflows.
- Supporting infrastructure providers.
- FORGE platform services.

---

## Information We Collect

409 Marketplace may collect information necessary to provide platform
services.

Categories may include:

### Account Information

Examples:

- User identity information.
- Authentication information.
- Account configuration data.

Purpose:

- Provide account access.
- Maintain account security.
- Support platform functionality.

---

### Financial Information

Examples:

- Financial institution connection references.
- Imported financial events.
- Account and transaction information provided through approved integrations.

Purpose:

- Provide financial reporting.
- Support user-authorized financial workflows.
- Generate application insights.

409 Marketplace does not access financial accounts without user authorization
through approved connection processes.

---

### Payment Information

Examples:

- Payment transaction references.
- Payment status information.
- Payment workflow records.

Purpose:

- Support payment processing.
- Maintain transaction history.
- Provide financial records.

Payment processing providers may process payment information according to
their own privacy and security practices.

---

### Technical Information

Examples:

- Application activity.
- Security events.
- Error information.
- System records.

Purpose:

- Maintain reliability.
- Protect systems.
- Improve platform security.

---

## How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide requested services.
- Authenticate users.
- Process authorized financial workflows.
- Maintain application security.
- Improve reliability.
- Investigate security events.
- Meet legal and operational requirements.

409 Marketplace does not use financial information for purposes unrelated to
authorized platform functionality.

---

## Financial Data Connections

Financial integrations require user authorization.

When users connect financial accounts:

- Users authorize the connection.
- Approved providers process connection requests.
- 409 Marketplace receives only information permitted through the authorized
  integration.
- Access credentials are handled through protected application boundaries.

---

## Third-Party Providers

409 Marketplace uses third-party providers to provide platform functionality.

Providers may include:

- Plaid.
- Stripe.
- Supabase.
- Vercel.
- GitHub.

Each provider maintains its own privacy and security practices.

409 Marketplace evaluates provider usage based on:

- Data handled.
- Required permissions.
- Security responsibilities.
- Business purpose.

---

## Data Protection

409 Marketplace protects information through:

- Authentication controls.
- Authorization boundaries.
- Owner-scoped data access.
- Credential separation.
- Secure development practices.
- Repository traceability.
- Security review processes.

Additional security improvements are tracked through the FORGE security
roadmap.

---

## User Rights and Requests

Users may request information regarding:

- Their account data.
- Their financial connections.
- Their stored application information.

Requests may be reviewed to verify identity and protect account security.
